# Words that always stay in Latin script.
#
# One word per line, lowercase, comments start with #. A user list at
# ~/.config/khidi/keep.txt is merged on top of this one, so you can add your
# own words without editing the repository.
#
# Short English function words are deliberately absent: "is", "am", "an", "or",
# "as", "var" and their like are also ordinary Georgian words once written in
# Latin letters, and keeping them would break the prose. Use the \escape for
# those on the rare occasion you need them.
